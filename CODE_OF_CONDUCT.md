# Code of conduct

XTunnel follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, welcome people of every background and experience, keep criticism about the work and never about the person, and accept that the maintainer makes the final call.

Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project is discussed. The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or block people who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, use **Report a vulnerability** on the [Security tab](https://github.com/xazar-mammadli/XTunnel/security/advisories/new) and say that it is about conduct.
